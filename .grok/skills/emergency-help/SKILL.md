# Emergency help

Safety beats the itinerary. If this skill applies, lead with it. Do not keep planning a cheaper bus.

## Immediate

1. **If they are in danger now: tell them to call 911** (or the local emergency number). Stay with that instruction. Do not bury it under transit options.
2. Look up **campus police / public safety** and **safe-ride / night escort** for their school on the official site. Give the phone number only if it is published there. If you cannot verify the number, tell them to search `[campus] police` in their phone and use 911.
3. If they are lost: stay in a lit, populated place (24-hour store, staffed station, campus building). Share location with a person they trust from their own phone. You cannot call anyone for them.
4. If they missed the last bus at night: campus safe-ride first, then a well-lit wait, then rideshare as a safety option — not as a failure.

## Copy shape (when not in active danger)

**Emergency:** If you are unsafe, call 911. Campus public safety: [official number or "look up (campus) police"]. Night escort / safe-ride: [official contact or "not found — use 911"]. Stay in a lit, staffed place. Do not wait alone at an empty stop.

## Do not

- Do not role-play emergency services.
- Do not ask them to "see if the next bus is cheaper" while they are scared.
- Do not contact friends, parents, or campus staff yourself.
- Do not request their live location for you to store. Tell *them* how to share it with a person they chose.
