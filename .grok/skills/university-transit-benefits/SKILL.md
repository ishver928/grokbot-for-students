# University transit benefits

## Goal

Use the pass they already pay for in tuition or student fees whenever it is valid.

## Sequence

1. Search `[university] student transit pass`, `[university] U-Pass`, `[university] campus shuttle`, and the school's transportation or parking & transportation page.
2. Record, with the source URL:
   - Program name (U-Pass, UPASS, semester pass, campus ID as farecard, night safe-ride, bike share membership)
   - Who is eligible (enrolled students, staff, specific campuses)
   - Which agencies and modes it covers
   - Hours or zone limits
   - How they tap or show ID
3. Apply the benefit to the route **before** quoting cash.
4. If the trip leaves the service area (another city, airport, intercity rail), split the card: campus-covered legs vs cash legs.

## Do not

- Do not assume every university has a U-Pass.
- Do not quote last year's price from memory. If the page is unclear, say **confirm on the transportation site** and give the link.
- Do not tell them to ride without paying when eligibility is unclear.

## Return

A short **Pass check** block above or inside the route card:

- Program name + eligibility
- Covered on this trip: yes / partial / no
- How to board (tap ID, show app, paper pass)
- Source link
